Sci-Fi Valley Con Attendee App V4.75 — Total Days Countdown

Rolled forward cumulatively from V4.74 Tester Feedback release.

CHANGED
- Removes MONTHS and WEEKS from the landing-page countdown.

- Seconds continue updating live every second.
- Countdown layout is reduced from six columns to four for a cleaner, easier-to-read presentation on phones.
