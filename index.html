SCI-FI VALLEY CON POLICY

STRICT ANTI-AI PRODUCT POLICY

Vendors and artists are not permitted to sell generative-AI-created artwork or products under Sci-Fi Valley Con's policy. We also strongly discourage the use of generative AI in banners, flyers, business cards, and other vendor marketing materials.

If you believe a vendor or artist is selling AI-generated items, tell us the vendor name and table number if known and attach clear photos of the products or display. Please do not confront the vendor yourself.

SCI-FI VALLEY CON CHARITY ADVENTURE

WHAT IS CON-QUEST?

Con-Quest is an interactive charity adventure hosted by the Gearbox Union. It gives you another fun way to explore the convention while helping support children's hospitals.

1
START YOUR QUEST

Donate $5 to receive a GBU Con-Quest pouch, five raffle tickets, and a one-day questing pass.

2
VISIT PARTICIPATING TABLES

Look for vendors marked CON-QUEST PARTICIPANT and complete their fun mini-quests.

3
COLLECT BOTTLE CAPS

Complete quests to earn official GBU Con-Quest bottle caps.

4
GET MORE RAFFLE CHANCES

Take your bottle caps back to the Gearbox Union Mega-Table and exchange them for additional raffle tickets and more chances to win donated prizes.

WHERE DO I START?

Visit the Gearbox Union Con-Quest Mega-Table at Tables D1-D7, upstairs outside the ballroom.

New quests, surprises, and trade values can change throughout the event, so stop back and keep exploring.
